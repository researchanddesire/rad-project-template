# R11 central hub assembly study

This is a proposed sequence for the one-piece/front-loaded prototype. It is
not a released build instruction. The current source is
`hardware/cad/front-load-retention-r11/candidate-c/FrontLoadRetentionR11.FCStd`.
Part profiles, materials, torque, actual cables and physical fit remain open.

1. Prepare the carrier and body electronics on the bench. The PCB keeper and
   motor/battery retention still need actual hardware/process acceptance.
2. With the nose cap off, feed the supported tail through the neck and load
   the carrier from the front. The rear head passes through the bore and
   finishes behind the metal collar. Twenty-one listed rigid poses clear;
   actual flexible-tail handling is not established by those samples.
3. Leave the flange PCB, covers and hub fasteners waiting. Bring the left
   hub half from negative X around the collar/head and cable space. Bring
   the right half from positive X. Each half has seven clear listed poses;
   the right-half check includes the seated left half.
4. Place the two nuts into the right-side pockets and support them until
   the screws start. Insert the two screws from the left. Five listed nut
   poses per nut and six screw poses per screw clear the stated context.
   These are nominal M2 × 20 screw and 4-AF × 1.6 mm nut envelopes, not a
   selected order list. No actual drive, formed thread, engagement or torque
   is qualified. Two assumed straight driver shafts clear; handles and grip
   are not represented.
5. Connect and mechanically retain the flange PCB island, then fit the covers
   and complete the stationary tip/cap sequence. **These remaining steps are
   not designed yet.** Do not use the cable to hold the board or flange.

The body collar and carrier head have separate nominal capture features.
Their finite reverse-motion probes demonstrate stops, not load ratings.
Carrier head webs are 1.35 mm front and 1.5 mm rear with complete material
witnesses. The electrical channel is a packaging space, not a finished cable.
Keep JP1 open and motor output locked for any later initial electrical build.

The goal remains a complete proposal for Lucy to consider building. Exact
materials, parts, PCB layouts, wing retention/compliance, tip support/wires,
seal/cover interfaces and first-build inspection criteria remain unfinished.
